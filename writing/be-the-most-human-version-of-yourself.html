Be the most human version of yourself

May 2026 · 1 min read

I’m leaving Miami today and while the LinkedIn posts from this week might suggest otherwise, the vibes were not good.

Most people I talked to are looking for a way out of their current situation.

They won’t say it upfront, but my gut is that underneath the unhappiness is a feeling that every single one of them is replaceable right now. These are people in tech roles being asked to train AI on their jobs, and we all know what comes after we train the AI. The call is coming from inside the house.

Running from your current situation isn’t going to solve what you’re actually feeling underneath it all... and a new job won’t fix it either. Unless you start a landscaping company (which I’m all for).

The real problem is that most people haven’t spent the time to figure out what they are best at doing, because it’s awkward and hard and most people avoid it.

AI should be taking things off your plate and clearing space for that important work. Who wants to spend hours reconciling reporting or fixing fonts in a deck anyway? Certainly not me. Let it do that and double down on the parts of you that robots can never replicate, because they will never be human and that is your advantage now and into the future.

So be the most human version of yourself, use AI to automate everything else, and watch what happens to that existential crisis... and your job satisfaction.
