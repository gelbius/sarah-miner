The goalpost keeps moving

May 2026 · 1 min read

Does anyone else have a constant, general feeling of FOMO right now?

I was on spring break with my kids last week, and even standing on a beach in paradise (Cabo San Lucas, in case you were wondering) I couldn’t fully turn my business brain off, and it wasn’t because I didn’t want to be present with my family, it was because things are moving so fast right now that stepping away for even a few days felt like falling behind. Maybe this is just the life of a founder but I have this feeling it has a lot to do with AI, too.

And it’s not just vacation that feels that way, it’s signing off at 5pm to have dinner, or going for a morning run instead of getting straight to work, there’s just this constant voice saying you could be building something right now, you could be learning something new, you could be moving faster while the window is open.

I’ve always been pretty good at seeing what’s coming up and actually moving on it when I see it, but right now the pace is so fast that by the time I get my arms around something it’s already evolved into something new, and I can’t tell if I’m ahead of it or behind it, which is a scary feeling that I’m not used to.

I remember when CARRY1 was getting close to hitting its first $100K in revenue and I was already thinking about $200K long before it even happened, and I think that’s the AI moment we’re all living in right now, where every milestone feels smaller than it should because the goalpost keeps moving.

Even Claude (my best friend and only true coworker) tells me to pause and celebrate my wins and milestones along the way, and my response every time is “but there’s still so much ahead!”
