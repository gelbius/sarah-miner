Promote for EQ, not just quota

March 2026 · 1 min read

Salespeople with high emotional intelligence produced twice the revenue of those without it.

(Hay Group study, 44 Fortune 500 companies.)

And yet when founders are deciding whether to promote their best rep to VP of Sales, EQ is almost never part of the conversation. Pipeline is. Quota attainment is. Relationships are.

Those things are measurable. EQ feels a bit fluffy.

But after almost 20 years joining, building and scaling sales teams, I’ve seen founders and CROs who got this hire wrong say the same thing afterward. They knew something was off. They promoted anyway.

The problem isn’t that EQ is hard to see. It’s that nobody tells you what to look for in the candidates.

I put together a framework to help. What EQ actually looks like in a sales context, what the red flags are before you make the call, how to interview for it and what to ask references.

If you’re about to make this decision, swipe through before you do.
