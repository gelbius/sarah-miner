About

I come in as an operator, not a consultant.

Founder and CEO of CARRY1. Ten years at Reddit, helping take the ads business from zero to $1B. Based in the New York City area.

The story

I joined Reddit in 2016 as the fifth sales hire in New York, back when there was no measurement, no case studies and a platform that still looked like Craigslist. Over the next ten years I helped scale the ads business from zero to $1B, from early startup through the IPO. I built and rebuilt sales teams, launched go-to-market for mobile, video and the site redesign, and finished as Head of Media, Entertainment + Sports. Some of the sellers I coached now lead revenue organizations of their own.

Before Reddit I spent more than five years in sales at The Walt Disney Company, where I managed the nine-figure AT&T multimedia upfront partnership tied to ESPN college football. Before that, I sold at Salesforce during its hyper-growth years.

The pattern I kept running into is a company where the product works and the audience is there, but revenue is still unpredictable. That’s the problem I solve through CARRY1. I come in, I listen to the team, I figure out what’s actually in the way, and then I do whatever needs to happen to fix it.

Along the way I’ve interviewed and hired hundreds of sellers and revenue leaders. If your product is ahead of your revenue organization, I’ve been where you are, and I can help fix it, fast.
