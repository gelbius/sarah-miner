Snow days never fall on a Saturday

March 2026 · 1 min read

Snow days never seem to fall on a Saturday.

I’ve been the default parent at home for seven years, even through the years when I had the most to lose professionally and a job that demanded everything. Back then, when I had to step away because my family needed me, I felt guilty. I assumed that guilt was about having a boss and about my commitment to a company that my kids were interrupting. I thought if I ever worked for myself, that feeling would for sure disappear…

Multiple times this winter, right in the middle of the week, major snowstorms hit. Our (incredible) nanny couldn’t get out to CT and Andrew had calls he couldn’t move, so I closed the laptop and baked cookies and practiced hockey drills in the living room. I wanted to be there. I always want to be there. But surprisingly, the same guilt I felt at Reddit was still there too and maybe even worse because CARRY1 is mine and a lost day really hurts.

I don’t think the guilt will ever go away. The guilt of leaving my kids for work or leaving my work for kids.

At the end of it all though, I’ll always be the one who closes the laptop when my family needs me. I’ll lean in and have the most fun when it happens because pretty soon, they won’t want me build a fort in the living room, fill it with snacks and put on Wreck-It Ralph.

I guess my new boss is just going to have to get used to it.
