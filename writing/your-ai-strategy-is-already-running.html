Your AI strategy is already running

June 2026 · 1 min read

Your AI strategy is already running. You just don’t know it yet.

I had a few conversations last month with senior people in revenue roles who told me things they would never post here.

One bought a $1300 personal laptop because her employer blocks the AI tools she wants to use. She’s ten years into a senior career, and she didn’t tell anyone she was doing it.

She isn’t the only one. The real AI conversation inside your company is happening on personal laptops at night, in side group chats and in private Slack messages.

If I were running a revenue team right now, I’d stop the “AI rollout”. I’d take the budget I was going to spend on the enterprise contract, divide it by the size of my team, and give every rep a monthly budget to figure out what works on their own. I wouldn’t tell them which tools to use.

Some of them will jump in immediately and start sharing what’s working. These are the builders, the ones who will grow with your company long into the future. They’re the people that will show you the right AI stack before you make the investment.

By the end of the quarter, you’ll have a real AI roadmap built by the people doing the work, not by vendors who have never met them before.

What’s the one AI tool you’d lose your mind over if your company banned it?
