From wedding rings to offer letters

July 2026 · 1 min read

I’ve set up three couples who are now married.

Lately, my matches have been ending in offer letters instead of engagement rings.

Today I’m launching Cardinal by CARRY1, a private, invite-only list of the consumer revenue and GTM operators I trust the most. When a company I believe in needs to hire, Cardinal will be the first place I look, and the people on the list will hear about roles before they’re posted.

I only take on companies I’d work for full time myself. The matches work because I’m already inside them as an operator, building the revenue foundation, so I know what it takes to be successful there before I put a candidate forward. The instincts come from ten years at Reddit, where I helped scale the ads business from start up to IPO, and hiring well was one of the most important parts of the job.

There’s no application and no waitlist, invitations come directly from me, and the first ones go out within the next week.
