Nobody is reading your priorities email

August 2026 · 1 min read

That email you spent all morning composing to guide your team on weekly priorities? They’re not reading it.

The Atlantic just reported that comprehension of multi-page text is declining across every age and education level. Most leaders feel this and are responding by writing longer, more detailed emails and documents.

So you’re writing more to make sure you’re understood, your sellers are dropping it into Claude to summarize it, and somewhere in that snackability, the best parts of your message fall out, like where you tell them they’re crushing it, or where you remind them to control what they can control.

The Claude summary hands them the to-do list and drops the “fluff”, but we all know sales teams need (and deserve) encouragement. We can’t afford to lose those moments.

Keep writing the email, but make it the resource they check later, not the way they hear your message first. Deliver the priorities live, then again in the one-on-one, and again next Monday. Before you get frustrated with me, having to say it multiple times isn’t a sales team problem (or a generational difference!), it’s life now, between Shorts and AI.

What’s the one message you find yourself repeating to your team every week?
