The ESPN years

February 2026 · 1 min read

Last night, we celebrated the many years we spent building at The Walt Disney Company, selling ESPN properties together.

It was an era when they were an absolute must-buy for anyone targeting men. It was when we launched Disney’s first streaming offering, WatchESPN. It was when we first started monetizing social. The work was very hard and the bar was (almost impossibly) high. We learned things like how to manage up effectively, what having strong executive presence meant, the importance of clear communication, and staying strong under pressure. We stayed in the office until the sun rose during our dreaded Upfront week.

This group got me through some of the hardest times in my career. We wouldn’t trade those times for anything. They laid the foundation for our future growth and gave us a lifetime of friendship.
