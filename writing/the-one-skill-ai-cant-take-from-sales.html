The one skill AI can’t take from sales

March 2026 · 1 min read

Anthropic just published data on which jobs AI will replace. Sales is on the list.

There is one thing you can develop that will keep your job safe.

I shadowed a call last week. The rep had done her prep work. She pulled the business objectives, knew the priorities, seemed ready. The SVP stopped her two minutes in. The data was from 2024 earnings. He had already moved on.

She couldn’t move with him. She had summarized the business without actually understanding it.

AI can pull the earnings report, write the call brief and generate the follow-up email. What it can’t do is read a room that changed direction.

That’s EQ. And, good news, the Hay Group found it can be developed. It’s not fixed at birth like so many people assume. It’s worth doing the work to build stronger emotional intelligence right now.

If you’re in leadership, I’m sure you’re thinking about how to automate sales right now. But an equally, if not more important, question is...

How are you building EQ on your team right now?
