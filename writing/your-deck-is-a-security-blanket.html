Your deck is a security blanket

June 2026 · 1 min read

According to a recent Forrester research study, 85% of your decision makers don’t want to talk to you.

They’ve already researched. They know what they need.

What’s actually working in sales right now: showing up with curiosity, listening first, diagnosing before you ever mention a solution, and then coming back with something tailored to what you actually learned about your client. I’m seeing this work well with teams that are building their entire go to market around this shift, moving away from the pitch and into the diagnosis.

Your deck is a security blanket. It lets you control the narrative, stick to the script, avoid the discomfort of a real conversation where you don’t know exactly what’s going to come up. Im sorry to say it but your clients don’t care about your offering, they care about their business, their challenges, their competitive position. Saying everything you have to offer doesn’t make them listen harder, it makes their eyes glaze over and they tune out.

The hardest part of your pitch is letting go of controlling the meeting because you’re terrified of what happens when you don’t have a script and you can’t predict where the conversation is going to go. But, that fear is exactly why you’re losing.

What would change if you walked into that first meeting thinking you were there to learn, not teach?
